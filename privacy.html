FACET

Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026

The short version. Facet has no account and no server holding your data. Your scan photos and face analysis are processed on your phone and stay there. We don't run ads, we don't track you, and we don't sell anything about you. The only data that leaves your phone is the record of your purchase, which Google Play and our subscription provider, RevenueCat, need to manage your subscription.

Who we are

Facet ("we", "us") is published by Gourav Bhardwaj, India. Contact us about privacy at iaamgourav@gmail.com.

Information stored only on your phone

To build and track your plan, Facet stores the following in the app's private storage on your device. We never receive it.

  • Your first name and birth year.
  • Your onboarding answers, such as goals, daily time, habits, skin answers and safety answers.
  • Your plan, completed routines, streak and reminder settings.
  • Scan photos, the face landmark points detected in them, and the marker numbers calculated from them (puffiness, contour, skin clarity and posture angle).

Camera and face analysis

Facet uses your camera only when you take a scan. The photos are analysed on your device by face and pose detection models built into the app. This works offline, and no image, landmark or measurement is uploaded.

Facet does not identify you, recognise faces, compare you with other people, or create biometric identifiers. The landmarks are used only to calculate your own markers, which are compared only with your own earlier scans. Photos that fail the quality check, and the camera preview frames, are deleted immediately.

Backups

Android can back up app data to your Google account. Facet leaves scan photos out of cloud backup. Your plan, progress and settings may be included, so they can be restored if you reinstall. That backup is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings. If you transfer to a new phone directly from your old one, your photos move across too.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment card or billing details.

We use RevenueCat to check and manage your subscription. RevenueCat receives your purchase history, which covers the products, dates, renewals, cancellations and refunds. It also receives a random app user ID created on your device and your store locale and currency. RevenueCat acts as our service provider, and the data is encrypted in transit. We use it only to unlock Facet Pro, handle renewals, trials and refunds, and see how many people subscribe.

Notifications

Reminders, including the reminder sent before a free trial ends, are scheduled on your device. Facet doesn't use push notification servers.

What we don't do

  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking SDKs, and we don't share data with data brokers.
  • We don't sell or rent personal information.
  • We don't collect your location, contacts, messages or files.

Age

Facet is for people aged 18 and over. The app asks for your birth year and doesn't continue for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • In the app: Privacy Centre → Delete all photos removes your scan photos. You → Delete all my data removes everything Facet stores on your phone.
  • Uninstalling Facet deletes its data from your phone.
  • Purchase records: email iaamgourav@gmail.com and we'll delete your customer record from RevenueCat. Google Play keeps its own transaction records under Google's policy.

Privacy Centre → Export my data lets you share a copy of your profile, plan, progress and scan results (without photos) at any time. Where it goes is your choice.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example under the GDPR or UK GDPR, California privacy law, or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your personal data, and to object to or restrict its use. Almost all of your data is already under your control on your device. For anything we or RevenueCat hold, contact us and we'll respond within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Where the GDPR applies, the legal basis for processing purchase data is performing our contract with you, which means providing the subscription you bought.

Security

Your data stays in Facet's private app storage, which other apps can't read and which modern Android devices encrypt. Purchase data is sent over encrypted connections.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. If the change is significant, such as collecting new kinds of data, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
